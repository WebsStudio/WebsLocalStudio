## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-modern, high-performance dark aesthetic tailored for digital craftsmanship. It bridges Mediterranean boutique approachability with tier-one engineering authority. 

### Core Personality
- **Technological & High-Caliber:** Clean, purposeful interactions with subtle electric blue luminous accents that evoke speed, security, and digital expertise.
- **Architectural & Elegant:** Deep midnight navy layers instead of stark monochromatic blacks, offering depth, optical warmth, and luxury.
- **Approachable Craftsmanship:** Crisp, structured layouts that speak clearly to local business owners while delivering agency-grade polish.

### Style Movement
The system balances **Dark Glassmorphism** with **Precision Minimalism**. It pairs deep blue-black tinted background layers, diffused ambient glow dynamics, thin translucent structural borders (`border-white/10` and `border-cyan-500/20`), and high-readability typography to create a high-value, tactile digital atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive oceanic dark matrix accented with hyper-vibrant optical glows.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - Base canvas: `#0A0F1D` (Deep oceanic void).
  - Surface Tier 1 (Cards, panels): `#111C35`.
  - Surface Tier 2 (Elevated modules, modals): `#162244`.
  - Surface Tier 3 (Interactive hover states, active pill fills): `#1E2E56`.
- **Primary Accent (Electric Blue):** `#0066FF` and `#2563EB`. Drives core CTAs, focus indicators, and key converted links.
- **Tertiary Accent (Cyan / Sky Glow):** `#38BDF8`. Reserved for technical highlights, badge borders, and luminous drop-shadow gradients.
- **Secondary Accent (Emerald WhatsApp / Live Status):** `#10B981`. Dedicated to instant communication touchpoints, conversion guarantees, and uptime/availability pulses.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - High-emphasis text: `#F8FAFC`.
  - Mid-emphasis body text: `#CBD5E1`.
  - Low-emphasis metadata / captions: `#94A3B8`.
  - Structural stroke lines: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and structural anchors to instill contemporary, geometric precision with welcoming European warmth. **Inter** handles all body copy, forms, and data metadata for uncompromised legibility across dense project specs, pricing grids, and analytical proofs.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) and high contrast `#F8FAFC`. Used for value propositions and sector positioning.
- **Section & Card Titles:** Bold weights ensure immediate scanning for business owners reviewing service packages and case studies.
- **Body Text:** Neutral-tinted `#CBD5E1` prevents visual fatigue on pitch-black screens while maintaining WCAG AAA contrast ratios.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural foundation relies on a 12-column fluid grid system with a maximum centered container width of `1280px`. 

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), page canvas margin `2.5rem` to `4rem`. Generous breathing room between editorial showcase blocks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px), canvas margin `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px), canvas margin `1.25rem` (20px). Grid modules stack vertically into uninterrupted full-width story flows.

### Spacing Scale Rules
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal pill tags, micro badge padding, and icon-to-label gaps.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for form inputs and standard stacked card elements.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card interior padding and module clusters.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) to delineate secondary blocks, metric proof counters, and interactive comparison steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses atmospheric layered darkness accented by subtle illumination flares, rather than heavy opaque drop-shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#0A0F1D`):** The foundational substrate. Holds decorative SVG mesh grids and low-opacity radial gradients (`rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 1 - Standard Card Surface (`#111C35`):** Finished with a razor-thin 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- **Layer 2 - Elevated Hover & Modal (`#162244`):** Employs an electric blue ambient wash: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.22), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.

### Luminous Borders & Glass
- Glass panels apply a backdrop filter of `blur(16px)` over `rgba(17, 28, 53, 0.75)`.
- Cards under active or hover status trigger an animated gradient line or static `border-color: rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)` with a subtle cyan glow (`0 0 14px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape geometry (`roundedness: 2`), delivering a modern, friendly tech presence that softens technical edges without descending into overly informal or playful shapes.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Inputs, Buttons, Badges (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Tactile, comfortable targets for desktop cursor precision and thumb navigation on mobile.
- **Feature Cards, Panels, Modals (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem):** Generous rounded curves that frame project visuals and service metrics seamlessly.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for live badges, WhatsApp quick-action triggers, and categorical taxonomy filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric Glow):** Background `#0066FF` transitioning to `#2563EB` on hover. Text `#F8FAFC`, font weight 600. Dynamic outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.4)`. Radius `rounded-lg` (16px), height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`.
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Background `rgba(22, 34, 68, 0.7)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`. Text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `border-color: #38BDF8`, background `#1E2E56`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Background `#10B981` with ambient emerald diffusion `0 4px 18px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`. Bold white label with the official SVG icon.

### Cards & Service Modules
- **Container:** Background `#111C35`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, radius `rounded-xl` (24px), padding `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Interactivity:** On mouse enter, border shifts smoothly to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)` accompanied by a faint radial cyan highlight originating from the card header.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Surfaces:** Background `#0D1527`, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Height 48px, padding `0 space-md`, radius `rounded-lg`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#38BDF8`, zero-offset ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Live Indicator Badge:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`. Contains an emerald 8px dot with a continuous ping animation, paired with `#10B981` label text.
- **Tech Stack & Service Chips:** Pill shape, background `#162244`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#CBD5E1`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Box:** Width/Height 20px, radius 6px (checkbox) or circle (radio), background `#0D1527`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)`.
- **Checked State:** Background `#0066FF`, border-color `#38BDF8`, crisp `#F8FAFC` checkmark icon.

### Portfolio Showcase Viewport
- **Device Mockup Frame:** Wrapped in a sleek `#162244` chassis with an ultra-thin highlight `border-t-white/20`, casting an ambient drop shadow into the `#0A0F1D` canvas to give local client projects tangible prestige.
